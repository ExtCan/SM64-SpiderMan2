Mario Mode needs your own copy of Super Mario 64 (USA).

Put the ROM in THIS folder. Any of these works:
  - .z64 (big-endian), .n64 (little-endian) or .v64 (byte-swapped)
  - any file name; sm64.us.z64 is checked first

Only the unmodified US version is accepted:
  SHA-1 (as .z64) 9bef1128717f958171a4afac3ed78ee2bb4e86ce
The Japanese, European and Shindou versions are recognised and rejected
with a message in sm2mario.log.

Mario's textures, animations, voice and sound effects are read from this ROM
at start-up. Nothing from it is copied anywhere else.

This folder (<game>\sm2mario) is yours: Overstrike deletes and recreates the
game's "scripts" folder every time you press "Install mods", but it never
touches this one. Your settings (sm2mario.ini), binding overrides
(bindings.user.ini) and the log (sm2mario.log) live here too.
